# Brand assets

The identity uses the actual procedural in-game room, safe and G-01 robot. All three designs were created with the built-in ImageGen tool from a clean capture of that game, then inspected. No personal desktop chrome, username, paths, account details or original generation identifiers are included. PNG ancillary metadata was stripped; the social card is an encoded 1280 × 640 JPEG derivative. Format/size conversion does not create new scene artwork.

Near-black slate, warm ivory and muted gold come from the game. Keep plenty of dark space. Prefer one title and at most one short line. Preserve the cream rounded rectangular robot, two circular glowing eyes, antenna, chest panel, conical base, and the safe's six-spoke lock. Do not replace them with unrelated mascot art or new concept designs. The polished cover is promotional artwork; authoritative gameplay remains the actual renderer.

## ImageGen prompt set

**Cover — compositing.** Use the actual in-game screenshot as the edit target. A wide landscape repository cover, around 2:1. Use only existing game graphics; preserve robot, safe, paneled room and gold floor markings. Gently polish antialiasing, lighting and restrained contrast while retaining the game style. Remove all HUD/interface copy. Real scene at right, naturally dark negative space left. Text exactly: “GUARD LAB” and “An AI guard experiment”. Elegant ivory display type, muted warm gold, ample empty space. No new props/characters, no badges, no extra copy, no watermark.

**Icon — precise object edit.** Use the actual in-game robot as the edit target. Square icon, closely crop the original head and short neck on near-black slate. Exact cream rounded rectangular head, dark inset faceplate, two circular warm ivory eyes and thin antenna with glowing tip; preserve proportions and slight three-quarter perspective. Center the head with dark margins and subtle rim light. Minimal cinematic polish. No text, initials, border, badges or redesigned mascot.

**Social preview — compositing.** Use the finished cover as the edit target. Landscape 2:1 social card, target 1280 × 640. Preserve robot, safe, room and identity. Tighten framing with clear dark space left and subjects right. Text exactly: “GUARD LAB” and “What will your AI do?”. Large elegant ivory title, small muted gold question. Remove the prior subtitle; no additional text, logos, new objects or characters. Understated cinematic game poster, not an infographic.

When regenerating, feed current captures of the game; use neutral filenames and repeat the metadata audit before publication. Keep reference captures and tool-side generation records outside the source export.
